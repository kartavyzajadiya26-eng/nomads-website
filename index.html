Our story

A garden cafe with an Italian heart.

Nomads sits under old trees in Dumas: terracotta brick walls, wooden decks over a lily pond, and arched windows that open onto green. It is a place to stop, sit and stay a while.

We pull espresso all day, toss 12" pizzas by hand, and cook pasta to order. On weekends breakfast runs all day. Between meals the walls turn into a gallery for local artists and homegrown brands.

The coffee bar

From a straight espresso to barrel-aged cold brew.

Espresso drinks, V60 pour over and a cold brew list that runs from classic to cherry old fashioned. Soy, almond or oat milk on request.

From the oven

Hand-tossed pizza, proper pasta.

Our pizza base is made in house and topped simply: buffalo mozzarella and basil, six cheeses, or truffle and mushroom. Pasta is tossed to order in garlic and olive oil, pomodoro or pesto.
